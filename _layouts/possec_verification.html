What this package checks

Symbolic curvature identities, decomposable-plane algebra, coefficient bounds and boundary formulas, plus directed-rounding checks of specified scalar inequalities. Exported polynomial certificates can be replayed independently with exact rational arithmetic; interval checks require Sage.

A PASS belongs to its stated check—not automatically to the full theorem.

≠

What a passing run does not prove

The global theorem is not machine-certified end to end. Smooth topology, geometric interpretation, uniform analytic estimates and external theorems still require mathematical arguments. This is not a Lean formalization.

Unverified by this computation does not mean false; it means outside its certified scope.
